Last Weekend at the Madring

The last race weekend was held at the new circuit in Madrid, Spain. And it was not as chaotic as expected.

Before the race, the track was labeled a "Car Killer" due to it's street circuit artibutes and high speeds streches.

Engineers were forced to run high downforce setups to cope with the straights, but aslo comprimise for the floor scraping Turn 12 La Monumental.

Lando Norris secured Pole Postion on Saturday for Mclaren ahead of championship Leader Kimi Antonelli.

The race was ultimately won by Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli, who benefitted from the Virtual Safety Car with Lance Stroll, securing race win from Lando Norris.
